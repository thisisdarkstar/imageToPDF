💡 What is Image to PDF?

Image to PDF is a modern, high-performance Android application that turns single or multi-page photos into PDF documents directly on your device. Everything — filtering, rotation, reordering, watermarking, and PDF rendering — happens locally in memory. The app does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere, even in principle.

⚡ How it works

Add pages

Snap with the in-app camera or pick photos from the system Photo Picker.

Enhance & reorder

Rotate, crop, apply B&W or Magic Color, then drag to arrange the sequence.

Configure PDF

Choose page size, orientation, margins, quality preset, and optional watermark.

Export

Save to Downloads, share, or print — all directly on device.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Image to PDF is engineered with an uncompromising privacy architecture:

  • No Internet Access — the android.permission.INTERNET permission is not declared in the manifest, making remote communication physically impossible.
  • 100% On-Device Processing — all image enhancement, binarization, layout, and PDF generation run in your device's local memory.
  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry — no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or crash report beacons.
  • Scoped Storage — modern MediaStore APIs; no broad external storage permissions required on Android 10+.
