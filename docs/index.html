💡 What is Image to PDF?

Image to PDF is a modern, high-performance Android application that converts single or multi-page photos into PDF documents directly on your device. Everything — filtering, rotation, reordering, watermarking, and PDF rendering — happens locally in memory. The app does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere, even in principle.

✨ Features

📸 Easy Ingestion

Multi-image Photo Picker, in-app camera capture, and batch selection from dozens of images at once.

💎 Lossless Rendering

Zero lossy downscaling. Quality presets: Ultra HD (100%), Balanced (85%), and Compact (65%).

🎨 Document Filters

Original, B&W Clean, Grayscale, Magic Color, and Sepia — with pinch-to-zoom, pan, and fit/fill crop.

📐 Page Layout

A4, US Letter, or exact Fit-to-Image sizes with Portrait, Landscape, and Auto orientation plus adjustable margins.

🛡️ Watermark Stamps

Native canvas-rendered CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, and custom stamps in diagonal (45°) or horizontal alignment.

🖨️ Export & Print

Save to Downloads, native Android printing, sharing, and an offline searchable document history.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Image to PDF is engineered with an uncompromising privacy architecture:

  • No Internet Access — the android.permission.INTERNET permission is not declared in the manifest, making remote communication physically impossible.
  • 100% On-Device Processing — all image enhancement, binarization, layout, and PDF generation run in your device's local memory.
  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry — no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or crash report beacons.
  • Scoped Storage — modern MediaStore APIs; no broad external storage permissions required on Android 10+.
