Privacy Policy

Application: Image to PDF · Last Updated: September 18, 2026

🛡️ Summary of our privacy philosophy
  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, store, or transmit your photos, documents, or personal data.
  • No Internet Permission: The app does not declare or request internet access (android.permission.INTERNET). It is physically impossible for the app to send data to any remote server.
  • 100% Offline Processing: All image enhancements, binarization, reordering, and PDF rendering happen locally in device memory.
  • Zero Analytics & Third-Party Trackers: No advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or diagnostic beacons are present.
1 Information we collect

None. Image to PDF is built strictly under the principle of Zero Knowledge and Privacy by Design. We do not require account creation, registration, email signups, or personal identity information of any kind.

Your documents, images, filenames, metadata, and generated PDFs remain exclusively on your device and are never monitored or analyzed.

2 Device permissions used

To provide core document scanning functionality, Image to PDF requests the minimum necessary permissions in compliance with Google Play's Principle of Least Privilege:

Permission Status Specific Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA Optional Allows you to snap photos of paper documents, receipts, or notes directly within the app. Camera photos are processed in temporary local cache and never transmitted.
Storage Access Scoped · PhotoPicker The app uses the standard Android System PhotoPicker to select images without requiring broad storage permissions. Generated PDFs are kept in your private app space or exported directly to the public Downloads/ImageToPDF/ directory via Android MediaStore.
android.permission.INTERNET Not Requested The app operates completely offline with zero network connectivity.
3 Google Play Data Safety declaration

In adherence to Google Play Console Data Safety requirements:

  • Data collected: "No" — the application does not collect user data.
  • Data shared: "No" — the application does not share user data with any third party.
  • Security practices: All data stays within Android's sandboxed local application storage.
4 Third-party libraries & services

The application only utilizes official Google Jetpack AndroidX components and Coil for local memory bitmap decoding. No third-party ad networks, social logins, or behavioral analytics frameworks are integrated into this software.

5 Children's privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Because our application collects zero personal information from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families policy. It is safe for all age demographics.

6 Data retention and deletion

Since your documents and history exist solely on your physical device, you retain 100% control over them at all times:

  • You can delete individual PDF records from the in-app History screen with a single tap.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling the application removes all locally stored temporary files and preferences immediately.
